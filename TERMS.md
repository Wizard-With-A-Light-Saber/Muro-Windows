## 2. What Muro does to your PC

Muro puts video or interactive wallpapers behind your desktop icons and, if you choose, on the lock screen, as the screen
saver and as the Windows accent colour. It can start with Windows and handle `muro://` links. You control each of these in
Settings, and Muro puts your own wallpaper, lock screen picture and colour back when you turn a feature off, quit Muro or
uninstall it. Wallpapers use some CPU, GPU and memory, and interactive wallpapers use more. Muro tells you the cost of its
optional effects before you turn one on.
